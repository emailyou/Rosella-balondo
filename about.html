About Me

My name is Rosella Jhyne Z. Balondo, and I am a college student currently taking Bachelor of Science in Business Administration major in Financial Management at Dr. Aurelio Mendoza Memorial Colleges (AMMC).

At this stage of my life, I consider myself an independent woman who is learning how to handle responsibilities and face challenges on my own. Every day, I commute to college, which has taught me to be more responsible, patient, and mindful of my time. Although commuting and balancing my responsibilities can sometimes be challenging, I continue to do my best because I know that every effort brings me closer to my dreams.

I may not be a sporty person, but I have my own strengths and interests. I believe that success does not depend on being good at sports or having the same talents as other people. What matters most is knowing your own abilities, being willing to learn, and never giving up on your goals.

I value my education and my family because they are two of the most important parts of my life. My family inspires me to work hard, while my education gives me hope for a brighter future. I know that life will not always be easy, but I am willing to face difficulties and learn from my experiences. I want to become a better version of myself and prove that, through determination and hard work, I can achieve the things I dream of.
